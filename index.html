Questions

FAQ

Is mint open?

No. This is a preview — no contract is deployed and the mint button is disabled. The launch date will be announced on @coineko_ (time in UTC).

How does the free mint work?

Your wallet’s first mint is free: you pay 0 BNB, only network gas (about $0.01). Every mint after that costs exactly 0.001 BNB, up to 10 coins per wallet in total.

Who can mint?

Any wallet that signs its own transaction — including an AI agent wallet such as Binance Agentic Wallet. Contracts cannot mint (msg.sender must equal tx.origin). There is no .ai Agent ID gate.

What is the MNC bonus?

Each paid mint earns 120,000 MNC (coineko, BEP-20), claimable on-chain with claim(). Free mints earn 0. Max 1,080,000 MNC per wallet. MNC is a mint bonus only — not a promise of value, yield, or returns.

What happens to the old CoinEko (1,111)?

It is a separate collection with its own contract, and it is not touched by this edition. When this 8,888 edition launches, it takes over coineko.fun; the old 1,111 page stays available on Vercel only.

Is this a Binance collaboration?

No. Maneki Neko Coin is an independent project. Binance Agentic Wallet is one wallet option for minting, not a partnership or endorsement.
