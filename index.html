Questions

FAQ

Is mint open?

Yes — mint is live on BNB Chain. First mint per wallet is free, then 0.001 BNB each, up to 10 per wallet.

How does the free mint work?

Your wallet’s first mint is free: you pay 0 BNB, only network gas (about $0.01). Every mint after that costs exactly 0.001 BNB, up to 10 coins per wallet in total.

Who can mint?

Any wallet that signs its own transaction — including an AI agent wallet such as Binance Agentic Wallet. Contracts cannot mint (msg.sender must equal tx.origin). There is no .ai Agent ID gate.

What is the MNC bonus?

Each paid mint earns 120,000 MNC (coineko, BEP-20), claimable on-chain with claim(). Free mints earn 0. Max 1,080,000 MNC per wallet. MNC is a mint bonus only — not a promise of value, yield, or returns. Note: the NFT itself is also called MNC (Maneki Neko Coin) — the NFT and the bonus token share the ticker but are two different contracts.

Why would my mint fail?

Almost always the amount. Send exactly 0 for a wallet’s first mint and exactly 0.001 BNB for each after; overpaying also reverts. Read priceFor(address, quantity) first. Also check the wallet has not reached 10, and that you are on BNB Chain (56).

What happens to the old CoinEko (1,111)?

It is a separate collection with its own contract, and it is not touched by this edition. This 8,888 edition has taken over coineko.fun; the old 1,111 page stays available on Vercel only.

Is this a Binance collaboration?

No. Maneki Neko Coin is an independent project. Binance Agentic Wallet is one wallet option for minting, not a partnership or endorsement.
